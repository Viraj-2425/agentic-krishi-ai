What it actually does

Six jobs, one conversation.

No dashboards to learn. KrishiMitra fits into how a farm already talks — a phone call, a photo, a voice note — and answers in kind.

Crop doctor

Send a photo of a sick leaf. Get the disease or pest named, and the treatment window that actually matters.

Mandi prices

Live rates from nearby markets, with a plain sell-or-hold call — not just a number to interpret yourself.

Weather & irrigation

Forecasts tied to your field's coordinates, so spraying and watering are timed to what's actually coming.

Scheme navigator

Matches you to subsidies and schemes you already qualify for, and fills the paperwork for you to check.

Voice-first

Works over a normal phone call in your own language. Typing is optional, never required.

Soil & sowing plan

Rotation and sowing windows built from your soil test and last three seasons of yield, not a generic almanac.
